PROJECT TITLE
AI Food Waste Predictor System
PROJECT OVERVIEW
The AI Food Waste Predictor System is a web-based application that uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze food consumption patterns and predict potential food waste. The system helps households, restaurants, and organizations reduce food wastage by providing insights based on factors such as quantity purchased, consumption rate, storage conditions, and expiry dates.
PROBLEM STATEMENT
Food waste is a major global issue affecting both the economy and the environment. Large amounts of food are wasted daily due to poor planning, over-purchasing, and lack of awareness. Traditional methods do not provide accurate predictions or preventive suggestions. Therefore, an AI-based Food Waste Predictor System is required to analyze food usage and predict waste in advance.
PROJECT OBJECTIVE
Main Objective:
To develop an AI-based system that predicts food waste and helps users take preventive actions.
Specific Objectives:
Collect food consumption data.
Analyze usage patterns using AI algorithms.
Predict potential food waste.
Provide alerts before food gets wasted.
Suggest ways to reduce food waste.
Improve sustainable food management.

INPUT PARAMETERS
Food Item Name
Quantity Purchased
Purchase Date
Expiry Date

Consumption Rate

OUTPUT
Low Waste Risk
If the food is likely to be consumed before expiry.
High Waste Risk
If the food is likely to be wasted due to low consumption or nearing expiry.
MODULES

Module 2: Food Data Collection
Collects details about food items and usage.
Module 3: Data Validation

Module 4: AI Waste Analysis
Analyzes patterns using machine learning.
Module 5: Waste Prediction
Predicts the risk level of food waste.
Module 6: Alert & Dashboard
Displays results and sends alerts.
